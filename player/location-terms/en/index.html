Article 4 (Details of the Service and Fees)

① The Company provides the following location-based services using the GPS location information of the User's device.

  • Display of the game map (Exploration View) around the current location
  • Generation (spawning) of game objects such as Gates and wild monsters based on the current location
  • Location-based verification of game results such as hunting rewards (prevention of cheating)
  • Installation of game facilities (Guild HQ, Monster Lure Device, etc.) at coordinates designated by the User, and display thereof to other Users
  • Location sharing between friends and guild members (only where the User has separately consented to and enabled this function in the app)
  • Display of activity areas on the Player Globe and elsewhere: the areas in which the User has been active in the game (areas passed through, areas where Gates were raided, etc.) are recorded in units of areas and, only where the User has separately turned on "Allow recording my trail" in the app, are displayed on the globe on the Company's website (utobit.net/player/globe) and elsewhere

② Use of the location-based service itself is free of charge. However, some in-game content is paid (in-app purchases), and matters relating thereto are governed by the Terms of Service and the Purchase Withdrawal Guide. Data communication charges incurred in the course of the collection and use of location information are borne by the User.

Article 5 (Purpose and Period of Retention of Personal Location Information)

① The Company uses personal location information only for the purpose of providing the services under Article 4 and, in principle, uses it for that purpose immediately upon collection and does not store it thereafter. Location information is collected only while the app is in use (in the foreground) and is not collected in the background.

② Notwithstanding paragraph ①, the following information, which the User posts by designating coordinates himself or herself through game functions, is not personal location information but game content that the User has chosen to make public, and is displayed to all Users for as long as the relevant function is maintained.

  • Guild HQ: the coordinates of the building designated by the guild master at the time of establishment — public for as long as the HQ exists
  • Monster Lure Device: the coordinates of the device installed by the User — public for 2 hours after installation

③ Notwithstanding paragraph ①, the Company processes records of the areas in which the User has been active in the game (hereinafter "Activity Area Records") as follows.

  • What is stored: The areas in which the User has been active in the game, such as areas where Gates were raided and areas passed through (including the surrounding areas), may be stored as area numbers of approximately 5 km or larger in size. Precise coordinates and times are not stored. At present, areas where Gates were raided are stored for all Users at the time of the Gate raid, and areas passed through are stored for Users who have turned on "Allow recording my trail"; the types of activity stored may vary within the scope of this paragraph.
  • Display: Activity Area Records are displayed to anyone who searches by nickname or Hunter number on the Player Globe and elsewhere, only where the User has turned on "Allow recording my trail" on the "Manamuhan Studio" screen in the app. Before the permission is turned on, they are not displayed to anyone. When displaying them, the Company may group them into areas wider than the stored areas.
  • Retention and deletion: Activity Area Records are retained until withdrawal of membership. When the User turns off the permission, the Company immediately discontinues display and deletes the records up to that time without delay. Regardless of whether the permission is on or off, the User may request deletion of the records via the contact (email) set out in Article 2.

④ Where the User withdraws membership (deletes the account), the Company destroys without delay the personal location information it holds and the materials confirming the use and provision of location information. However, information that must be preserved under other laws and regulations is preserved for the period prescribed by those laws and regulations.

Article 6 (Retention of Materials Confirming the Use and Provision of Location Information)

In accordance with Article 16(2) of the Location Information Act, the Company automatically records and preserves materials confirming the collection, use and provision of location information in the location information system, and retains them for at least 6 months.

Article 7 (Provision of Personal Location Information to Third Parties and Notification)

① The Company does not provide personal location information to third parties without the User's consent, and where it provides a third-party provision service, it notifies the User in advance of the recipient and the purpose of provision and obtains the User's consent.

② Where the Company provides personal location information to a third party designated by the User, it immediately notifies the User, on each occasion, of the recipient, the date and time of provision and the purpose of provision. However, where the User has consented, notification may be given collectively within a period of up to 30 days.

③ The public game content under Article 5 ② (the coordinates of a Guild HQ and a Monster Lure Device), location sharing with friends and guild members to which the User has separately consented, and the display of activity areas separately permitted by the User (Article 5 ③) are matters in which the User himself or herself selects the scope of disclosure through game functions, and do not constitute provision to a third party under this Article.

Article 8 (Rights of Users)

① The User may at any time withdraw all or part of his or her consent to the collection, use and provision of personal location information. In such case, the Company destroys without delay the personal location information collected and the confirmation materials (limited to the part for which consent has been withdrawn).

② The User may at any time request the temporary suspension of the collection, use and provision of personal location information, and the Company does not refuse such a request and has in place the technical means necessary for it.

③ The User may request the Company to allow inspection of, or to give notice of, the following materials, and may request correction where there is an error. The Company does not refuse such a request without justifiable grounds.

  • Materials confirming the collection, use and provision of location information concerning the User
  • The reason for and details of the provision of the User's personal location information to a third party under the Location Information Act or other laws and regulations

④ Method of exercising rights: consent may be withdrawn by revoking the location permission in the device settings or by withdrawing from the account in the app (Menu → User → Delete Account); the display of activity areas may be withdrawn by turning off "Allow recording my trail" under "Visit Manamuhan Studio" on the app's sign-in screen; and requests for temporary suspension, inspection, notice or correction may be made via the contact (email) set out in Article 2.

Article 9 (Rights of Legal Representatives and of Persons Obligated to Protect Children Aged 8 or Under, etc.)

① Where the Company intends to collect, use or provide the personal location information of a child under the age of 14, it obtains the consent of the child's legal representative. The legal representative may exercise all of the User's rights under Article 8.

② Where a person obligated to protect a child aged 8 or under, an adult ward, or a person with a mental disability who falls under Article 26 of the Location Information Act consents to the collection, use or provision of personal location information for the protection of that person's life or body, the person concerned is deemed to have given consent. In such case, the person obligated to protect may exercise all of the User's rights.
