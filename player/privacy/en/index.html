  • Provision of pedometer-linked content (upon consent to the permission)
  • Provision of the Wear OS watch app linking function (when linked)
  • Diagnosis of service errors and service improvement
  • Essential notifications such as announcements and changes to the terms

Upon withdrawal of membership, the account and game progress data are deleted without delay. However, payment records may be retained separately for a certain period in accordance with applicable laws and regulations (such as the E-Commerce Act).

5. Processing of Location Information (in connection with the Location Information Act)

As a location-based service, Player : Final Raid processes personal location information in accordance with the Act on the Protection and Use of Location Information and the Location-Based Service Terms of Use.

  • Method of collection: The device GPS location is collected only while the app is in use (in the foreground) and is not collected in the background.
  • Purpose of use: Display of the game map (Exploration View), generation of Gates and wild monsters, and location-based verification of game results such as hunting rewards.
  • Retention period: In principle, location information is used for the above purposes immediately upon collection and is not stored thereafter. However, materials confirming the use and provision of location information under Article 16(2) of the Location Information Act are automatically recorded and preserved in the location information system.
  • Coordinate disclosure functions: The installation coordinates of a Guild HQ (for as long as the HQ exists) and a Monster Lure Device (for 2 hours after installation), which the user posts by directly designating coordinates through game functions, are game content that the user has chosen to make public and are displayed to all users. The fact that they will be made public is announced in the app prior to installation.
  • Location sharing with friends and guild members: Only where the user has separately consented to this function in the app (sharing toggle ON), the user's location is displayed to friends and guild members, and only while the user is online. Without consent, the location is not shared.
  • Activity area records: The areas in which the user has been active in the game, such as areas where Gates were raided and areas passed through (including the surrounding areas), may be stored as area numbers of approximately 5 km or larger in size (at present, areas where Gates were raided are stored for all users, and areas passed through are stored for users who have turned on the permission). Precise coordinates and times are not stored. These records are displayed on the Player Globe and elsewhere, viewable by anyone through a search by nickname or Hunter number, only where the user has turned on "Allow recording my trail" under "Visit Manamuhan Studio" on the app's sign-in screen (they may be displayed grouped into wider areas), and are not displayed to anyone before the permission is turned on. When the permission is turned off, display is discontinued and the records up to that time are deleted without delay, and the user may request deletion of the records at any time.
  • Withdrawal of consent: Consent may be withdrawn at any time by revoking the location permission in the device settings or by withdrawing from the account. If you do not consent to the collection of location information, use of location-based gameplay will be restricted.
