How to Delete Your Account (in the App)

  1. Launch the PLAYER app and sign in
  2. Open the menu (≡) at the top right of the main screen
  3. Select Settings
  4. Select the User tab in Settings
  5. Select Delete Account
  6. For confirmation, type "DELETE MY ACCOUNT" in the input field and confirm

If you have deleted the app or are unable to reinstall it, you may request deletion of your account at the email address below.

  • Email: clus0109@gmail.com (please state the email address used at registration or your nickname)

Data That Is Deleted

DataWhether deleted
Account information (email, UID, nickname)Deleted
Game progress data (level, items, skills, currency, clear records)Deleted
Payment (purchase) recordsDeleted after being retained for a separate period in accordance with applicable laws and regulations (such as the E-Commerce Act)

Retention period

  • Where linked to a Google account: the data is preserved for up to 30 days after withdrawal, and the account is restored if you sign in again within this period. It is permanently deleted once 30 days have elapsed.
  • Guest (unlinked) account: the data is permanently deleted immediately upon withdrawal and cannot be recovered.
