This Policy sets out the standards for withdrawal of purchase and refunds for paid products in the mobile game "Player : Final Raid" (hereinafter the "Service") provided by Utobit (hereinafter the "Company"). The "Purchase Withdrawal" button in the in-app Settings and System Shop links to this page.

1. Right of Withdrawal

In accordance with Article 17 of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, etc. (hereinafter the "E-Commerce Act"), a user may request withdrawal of purchase (a refund) within 7 days from the date of payment for a paid product (or the date on which the product was delivered).

However, withdrawal of purchase is restricted in the following cases (Article 17(2)5 of the E-Commerce Act, etc.).

  • Where all or part of the purchased currency (Crystals) has already been used (consumed)
  • Where the value of the currency has significantly decreased as a result of its use
  • Where, in the case of a product such as a membership whose period of use and effects apply immediately, use has commenced (in accordance with the standards in the table below)

Crystals are delivered to the account immediately upon purchase, and once they have been used and converted into items, currency or the like, provision of the content is deemed to have commenced and withdrawal of purchase may be restricted.

3. Defective Products, etc.

Notwithstanding Sections 1 and 2, where the content of a paid product differs from what was indicated or advertised, or the contract has been performed in a manner different from its terms, the user may withdraw the purchase within 3 months from the date on which the product was supplied, and within 30 days from the date on which the user became aware or could have become aware of that fact (Article 17(3) of the E-Commerce Act).

  • Refund request through your Google Play Store order history — all paid transactions in the Service are made through Google Play In-app Billing, and Google Play's refund policy and procedures take precedence for payments and refunds made through Google Play (requests within 48 hours after purchase, etc.). Once the refund is completed, the relevant product (and any currency and effects granted as a result of it) will be recovered.

5. Payments by Minors

Where a minor has made a payment without the consent of his or her legal representative, the minor or the legal representative may cancel the contract. If you wish to cancel, please contact us at the email address above.

Where the Service is terminated, the Company will give notice through an in-app notice and this page at least 30 days before the termination date, and unused paid currency and fixed-term products with a remaining period will be refunded in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
